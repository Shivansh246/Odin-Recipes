Maggie Recipe

Description

Maggie is nothing but noodles and they are the most popular in India. It is provided by Nestle. It is easy to make with according to choice no ingredients required and can be made in few minutes. Favoured by Indian mothers when they do not have the mood to make food.

Ingredients:

  • Maggie noodles
  • Maggie taste-maker
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Oil
  • Tomato Sauce(optional)

Steps:

  1. Take water in a steel pot or a pan and boil it
  2. Add maggie noodles to it and wait for sometime until the noodles become soft
  3. Drain some additional water but make sure to have a little more water to make it wet
  4. Add the taste-maker and salt according to your taste and also 1 tsp oil while continuously stirring
  5. Stir until the water becomes more thick
  6. The maggie is ready serve. Sauce can be added if wanted
