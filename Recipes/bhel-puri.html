Bhel-Puri Recipe

Description

Bhel-Puri is famous in India. It is a savoury combination of various mixtures like ‘Bhujia’, ‘Papdi’, ‘puffed-rice’, and many others. It is generally taken as a snack. In India mostly people eat it with adding additional healthy vegetables like ‘onions’, ‘tomato’ ,‘Green-Chilli’, and many others depending on person’s taste.

Ingredients:

  • Bhel-Puri Packet
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Green-Chilli
  • Chaat-Masala
  • Lemon

Steps:

  1. Chop all the vegetables
  2. Empty the bhel-puri packet and all chopped vegetables to the bowl
  3. Add Chaat-Masala and some salt
  4. Stir the mixture
  5. Add lemons according to the quantity and stir again
  6. Bhel-Puri is ready to serve
